What deletion removes

After ownership verification, support will remove the account’s Firebase Authentication identity and associated Firestore profile, transactions, budgets, teams and member records. This is a manual process, not an atomic server job. Profile-photo uploads and AI records are not part of this free-tier release. Support cannot erase offline data, screenshots or exports already stored on a device.

This small-group release has no guaranteed response or completion deadline. The publisher must monitor the support inbox and process verified requests. This page alone does not establish Google Play compliance. See the privacy policy for data handling.
