A few good questions.

How can I request early access?

Request early access with your email address. The publisher will personally email you if the APK becomes available. There is no automatic download or guaranteed delivery time. This website is a product preview, not a working browser finance app, and requesting access does not create an account.

Does it connect to my bank?

No bank links or money transfers. The core is about records you enter manually. A company salary entry is an expense record, not a payroll payment.

Who can see company records?

The company account owner. Firestore rules restrict records to the account owner; company records are not automatically shared with employees. Read about privacy and data.

How does account deletion work?

Contact pratikchittroda@gmail.com. Support verifies ownership and processes deletion manually. There is no guaranteed response or completion deadline. Prepare a deletion request.
