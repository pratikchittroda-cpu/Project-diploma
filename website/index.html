Two focused modes

Personal money and company finance stay distinct.

Personal For everyday finance
  • Personal dashboard with balance, income, and expenses.
  • Category budgets for food, transport, bills, shopping, and more.
  • Stats, transaction history, profile, settings, themes, and backup tools.
Company For business operations
  • Company dashboard with revenue, expenses, and cash flow.
  • Team management, department-aware transactions, and company reports.
  • Company profile, billing, subscription, settings, and business budgets.
